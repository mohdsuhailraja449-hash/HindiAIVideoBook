# 🎬 Hindi AI Video Book

Hindi AI Video Maker App - Create Hindi stories, scene-by-scene prompts and AI video ideas.

## 🚀 About the Project
**Hindi AI Video Book** ek aisi application ya project hai jo creators ko Hindi mein stories likhne, scene-by-scene prompts taiyar karne, aur AI video ke liye naye ideas sochne mein madad karti hai.

## ✨ Features
* 📖 **Hindi Story Generation:** Hindi mein kahaniyan aur scripts banane ke liye ideas.
* 🎥 **Scene-by-Scene Prompts:** Video generation ke liye alag-alag scenes ke acche prompts.
* 💡 **AI Video Ideas:** Naye aur trending video topics ke liye suggestions.
